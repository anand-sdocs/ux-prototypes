Start from a PowerPoint file

Drop a .pptx here, or choose one. Your slides appear as they are; then add merge fields, related-list tables, repeating slides and conditions on top.

  • Your deck’s design stays exactly as it is. Merge fields go into its text; everything else is saved beside it.
  • Change the design in PowerPoint any time and upload it again. Bindings follow the slides and shapes.
  • In this prototype the file stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Template JSON

S-Docs saves two things: the .pptx itself, with merge fields typed into its text, and this JSON for everything structural. Slides are keyed by PowerPoint’s slide ID and shapes by their shape ID, which survive edits and re-saves in PowerPoint. The renderer’s own model is never stored.
