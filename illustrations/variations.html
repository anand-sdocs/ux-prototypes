Illustrations — animation variations

Three takes on an "organizing / filing" narrative loop, built on the same corrected SVG.

A · Filing Sequence

Drawers slide open one after another in a steady wave, held folders bob gently out of phase, the checkmark draws in on each cycle. Ambient gears spin slowly underneath. Calm, continuous, good as a background loop.

B · Sync & File

The cloud folder pulses like a live sync indicator, the gear folder spins fast (actively processing), the checkmark flashes in on a quick cadence, and the arms sway gently as if presenting. Snappier, more "system is actively working" energy.

C · Assembly Wave

A single wave sweeps left to right every 4s: cabinet drawers pop in sequence, the gear-folder pulses, the person gives a small nod, the cloud-folder pulses, then the checkmark draws in — like a document visibly moving through a pipeline. Ambient gears spin continuously underneath.
