Capabilities

Five task types. Every document workflow you run.

Mix and match them into a single orchestrated agent — triggered manually, from Slack, on a schedule, by email, or by webhook.

Extract

Pull structured fields out of claim forms, invoices, contracts — any document that lands in an inbox, drive, or channel.

Generate

Produce real S-Docs documents — proposals, scorecards, statements of work — sourced and prioritized across your systems.

Analyze

Check extracted data against thresholds, or run a weighted playbook — IP protection, termination for convenience — against a document.

Save

File results into Salesforce, HubSpot, ServiceNow, or a spreadsheet — mapped to the fields your downstream systems already use.

Notify

Alert the right channel or inbox — conditionally, only when it matters — over Slack, Teams, or email.

Orchestrated, not scripted

Chain any of the five into one flow, insert steps anywhere, and trigger the whole thing manually, from Slack, on a schedule, by email, or by webhook — visually, like a flow designer.

How it works

From a sentence to a running agent.

Build it yourself, step by step — or describe it in plain language and watch it take shape.

1

Describe or design

Type what you want automated in a sentence, or build it manually — name, role, guardrails, tone, and caution level.

2

Connect your systems

Authenticate as yourself into Slack, email, Salesforce, HubSpot, Drive, Sheets — only the systems your org allows.

3

Orchestrate the tasks

Chain Extract, Generate, Analyze, Save, and Notify on a visual canvas, and set the triggers that kick it off.

4

Run, watch, govern

Monitor every run, step in when it asks, and let admins keep the whole thing inside policy.

Skills

Don't start from a blank page. Start from a skill.

A skill packages a ready-made playbook, extraction schema, or document target that any agent's task can attach — and an Analyze task can combine more than one. Author once, reuse everywhere.

  • Contract Review Skill — IP protection, termination for convenience, weighted into a risk score.
  • Invoice Extraction Skill — the standard fields off any vendor invoice.
  • Sales Proposal Generation Skill — deal terms and differentiators, pulled from the CRM.

Trust & governance

Creativity for your teams. Control for your admins.

Every capability above ships with the governance a large organization actually needs to turn it on.

Fine-grained RBAC

Roles control who can build agents, manage connectors, publish to the catalogue, or just run what's shared with them.

Connector allowlists

End users can only connect systems your admins have explicitly approved — enforced the same way for built-in and self-built agents.

Full audit trail

Every role change, connector policy update, and new connection is logged — visible to whoever holds audit access.

Human-in-the-loop

Agents can pause and ask before anything risky happens — confirmations queue up for a person to approve or reject.
